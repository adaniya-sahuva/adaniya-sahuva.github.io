The problem

Commission payouts were fully manual — nobody checked whether an agency’s trade licence or documents had expired before paying out.

What I analysed

Policy-based commission eligibility and promotion logic.

What I designed

An end-to-end payout flow: eligible agencies are tagged to a standard or promotional commission scheme, and a commission record is created in Salesforce.

What I implemented

Automated invoice emails and reminders to brokers, document updates through the broker portal, Finance and BD verification, then data moves to Oracle Fusion for payout.

Business impact

Only eligible agencies are paid, agency data stays up to date and no manual reconciliation is needed — a multi-week process now completes within a week, with 5+ agencies paid every week.
